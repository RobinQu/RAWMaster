PhotoVGG7.f32 is the trained waifu2x vgg_7 photographic noise2 model by nagadomi.
The model and its original implementation are available under the MIT license in LICENSE.txt.

RAWMaster converted the original JSON values to little-endian Float32 and reordered each
convolution for Metal Performance Shaders. The learned coefficients retain their values.
